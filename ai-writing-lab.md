# AI Writing Lab  
## Text-to-Image Pedagogy in Priority Education Zones

The **AI Writing Lab** is a pedagogical innovation developed within the IA_4_ZEP initiative. It uses generative AI as a **motivation and feedback tool** for descriptive writing in large, low-resource classrooms (60+ learners).

---

### 1. The Pedagogical Problem

Teaching descriptive writing in ZEPs faces three obstacles:
- Large classes make individual feedback impossible.
- Learners struggle to understand what makes a description "precise."
- Abstract writing exercises feel disconnected from learners' lived experience.

### 2. The Innovation: Write to See

The method is simple: **if the AI can generate the image, the description was clear. If it cannot, the description needs revision.**

**The workflow:**
1. **Corpus:** Learners study a literary character (e.g., Faydé from *Cœur du Sahel*, a prescribed Cameroonian text).
2. **Reading:** They identify physical traits, clothing, posture, expressions, and context.
3. **Writing:** Each learner produces a short descriptive text.
4. **Generation:** The teacher feeds each text into an AI image generator.
5. **Confrontation:** The generated image is compared with the learner's intention.
6. **Revision:** Learners refine their descriptions based on what the AI misunderstood.

### 4. Why This Works: Cognitive Foundations

This method operationalizes three concepts from the **Cognitive Trapeze Model**:

- **Short Loop (Learner–AI–Knowledge):** The AI generates a visual response to the learner's text — an immediate, tangible feedback loop.
- **Embodied Knowledge:** The abstract description becomes a concrete, visual, affective experience.
- **Pedagogical Curability:** Learners with language difficulties are supported by the visual outcome, which makes their progress visible.

---

### 5. Scalability & Open Access

This methodology is:
- **Replicable** across all grade levels and literary works.
- **Adaptable** to bilingual contexts (French / English).
- **Low-cost** — it requires only a teacher's smartphone or tablet.

All methodology, lesson plans, and anonymized learner productions will be released on **Zenodo** under a **CC-BY license**.
