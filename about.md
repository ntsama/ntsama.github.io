# About  
## Academic Profile & Research Identity  

I am a transdisciplinary researcher bridging **Philosophy of Language**, **Cognitive Science**, and **Computational AI for Education**. My core expertise lies in modeling the dynamic interplay between language, thought, and technology. I am the founder of the **Cognitive Trapeze Model**, a computational and pedagogical framework that integrates AI as a mediating actor in learning environments. My research is deeply grounded in empirical field work within Priority Education Zones (ZEP) in Cameroon, where I develop and test offline, low-resource AI tools to support neurodiverse and multilingual learners.

---

## Key Achievements

- **Theoretical Innovation:** Founder of the Cognitive Trapeze model — a computational framework formalizing language–thought co‑development into a predictive Bayesian network.
- **Field Impact:** Founder of IA_4_ZEP, a field initiative reaching **160 learners** and **60 teachers** across 4 Cameroonian ZEP schools.
- **Empirical Breakthrough:** First corpus-based evaluation of Whisper ASR models in real African ZEP classrooms (18 texts, 60+ learners, Apr–Jul 2026).
- **Public Sector Innovation:** Developer of SPS_DRES_AD, a public-sector educational data-management software deployed at MINESEC DRES Adamaoua.
- **Policy Impact:** Project Director of a GIZ-funded national project whose findings led the Cameroonian State to formalize volunteering activities.

---

## Career Path Note

My unconventional trajectory reflects a deep commitment to field research. After completing my five-year multidisciplinary training at the École Normale Supérieure of Yaoundé (2008–2013), I signed a ten-year statutory service commitment to the Cameroonian State and was deployed to a remote Priority Education Zone (Adamaoua), where I served continuously. These years are not research gaps: they are the **empirical foundation** of my research. I defended my PhD on 27/07/2022 while teaching in this ZEP, and the Cognitive Trapeze model directly results from a decade of systematic classroom observation. Having completed my statutory service, I am now fully available for full-time research and European collaborations.
