## Supervision & Research Team

The **"Cognitive Trapeze"** project is guided by a strong international team:

- **Primary Supervisor (Host Institution):** Prof. Daniel Spikol (University of Copenhagen, Denmark) — Associate Professor at DIKU and the Centre for Digital Education. Overall project leadership, supervision of WP1 (Bayesian formalization) and WP2 (empirical validation), and coordination of statistical analysis and consortium building.
- **Co‑Supervisor (Secondment):** Prof. Marcelo Worsley (Northwestern University, USA) — Leading researcher in AI-enhanced learning environments. Scientific supervision for WP3 and host of the 6‑month secondment (M10–M16) at the *tiilt lab*.
- **Field Supervision:** Regional Delegation of MINESEC (Adamaoua) and its Regional Inspectors (ICR) for IT and Languages — Oversee deployment, teacher training, protocol adherence, and data collection in the 4 pilot schools.
- **Academic Advisor:** Prof. Pius Ondoua (University of Yaoundé I, Cameroon) — Academic advisor providing theoretical grounding in philosophy of language and remedial pedagogy, ensuring philosophical coherence with empirical findings.
