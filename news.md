### **August 2026 — MSCA Proposal Fully Validated and Ready for Submission**

I am delighted to announce that my MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship proposal, **"Cognitive Trapeze"**, has been fully validated by my supervision team at the University of Copenhagen (UCPH) and the UCPH Research Support Office.

The final proposal (Part A, Part B-1, and Part B-2) has been reviewed and approved. It will be officially submitted to the **EU Funding & Tenders Portal** before the 9 September 2026 deadline, marking the culmination of over a year of preparation, technical proof-of-concept development, and international collaboration.

The project is supported by a strong supervisory team:
- **Primary Supervisor:** Prof. Daniel Spikol (UCPH, Denmark)
- **Co‑Supervisor & Secondment Host:** Prof. Marcelo Worsley (Northwestern University, USA)
- **Field Supervision:** MINESEC Regional Delegation (Adamaoua) and its ICRs
- **Academic Advisor:** Prof. Pius Ondoua (University of Yaoundé I, Cameroon)

With a total EU contribution of **€283,502** and a 24‑month timeline, the project aims to formalize the Cognitive Trapeze model and develop a 100% offline AI reading tutor for priority education zones in Cameroon. I look forward to the outcome of the evaluation process.
