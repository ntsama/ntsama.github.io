# IA_4_ZEP — Educational Resources  
## AI-Generated Comics & Digital Lesson Plans for ZEP Classrooms

The **IA_4_ZEP** initiative produces open educational resources to support guided reading in overcrowded priority education zones (60+ learners per class). Each resource is co-designed with teachers, aligned with the official Cameroonian curriculum, and optimized for projection in low-resource classrooms.

---

### 1. AI-Generated Comics (Bandes Dessinées IA)

To support the 5-step guided reading methodology, we produce **AI-generated comics** that visualize the tales studied in class. These comics serve as:
- A multimodal anchor for comprehension.
- A support for projection in classrooms with no individual textbooks.
- A bridge between the written text and the learner's imagination.

**Published Comic: *Tendani Ito — The Golden Canary***  
*9 panels • 6th grade French class • Based on an African folk tale*

### 2. Digital Lesson Plans

Each comic is accompanied by a **digitalized lesson plan** following the official 5-step methodology:
1. Situation of the excerpt
2. Reading and viewing
3. Elaboration of the reading grid
4. Confrontation
5. Synthesis (moral lesson)

**Example:** [Fiche 13 — Tendani Ito — Le Canari d'Or](/assets/pdf/Fiche_13_TENDANI_ITO_CANARI_D_OR.pdf)

- **Direct use:** 60 learners in 6th grade ZEP classrooms (Adamaoua, Cameroon).
