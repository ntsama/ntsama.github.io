### 2. Strategic Publication Roadmap (5 Axes – MSCA 2026–2029)

As part of the **Cognitive Trapeze** project, I have defined a strategic publication matrix aligned with my research axes to maximize scientific impact and contribution to Open Science.

**Axis 1 – Empirical, Neurodiversity & AI (Flagship Priority)**  
*Acoustic Signatures of Neurodiversity: Using ASR Error Cascades as Computational Biomarkers in Low‑Resource Classrooms*  
> Target: **IJAIED** (International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education) or **Computers & Education** (Q1)  
> Language: **English** (Technical & Medical impact)  
> Timeline: **September 2026** (co‑authored with Prof. Marcelo Worsley)

**Axis 2 – Theoretical Formalization & Computational Modeling**  
*From Philosophical Framework to Predictive Model: Formalizing the 'Cognitive Trapeze' as a Bayesian Network*  
> Target: **Minds and Machines** or **British Journal of Educational Technology** (Q1)  
> Language: **English**  
> Timeline: **M8** (Month 8 of the MSCA project)

**Axis 3 – Pedagogical Impact & Global South Context**  
*Evaluating the Impact of an Offline AI Tutor on Semantic Comprehension: A Controlled Pilot Study in Cameroonian ZEPs*  
> Target: **International Journal of Educational Development** or **Learning and Instruction** (Q1)  
> Language: **English**  
> Timeline: **M16 & M22** (Empirical validation results)

**Axis 4 – Epistemology, Didactics & Francophonie**  
*Le Trapèze Cognitif : De la triangulation pédagogique à l'IA comme actant d'apprentissage*  
> Target: **Revue Française de Pédagogie** or **Langage et Société** (Class A journals)  
> Language: **French** (Conceptual depth & Didactics)  
> Timeline: **Open (Post‑M8)**

**Axis 5 – Ethics, Educational Policy & Decoloniality**  
*Decolonizing AI in Education: Why Offline, Neurodiversity‑Aware Tutors are the Key to Bridging the Digital Divide*  
> Target: **AI & Society** or **Globalisation, Societies and Education** (Q1)  
> Language: **English**  
> Timeline: **Open (Post‑M22)**

### 3. Open Science & Preprint Strategy

To ensure immediate visibility and FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable), I systematically deposit all my preprints to:
- **arXiv** – for all technical, empirical, and English‑language articles (ASR, WER, LLM, Bayesian modeling).
- **HAL** – for all epistemological, didactic, and French‑language articles (Philosophy of language, Pedagogy).

All anonymized datasets (ASR corpus "Texts 1‑19") and source code are deposited on **Zenodo** with a dedicated DOI and released under an **MIT / CC‑BY** license.
