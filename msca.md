# MSCA Project  
## Cognitive Trapeze: Modeling Language–Thought Co‑Development in AI‑Augmented Learning  
*A Computational Approach to Literacy Acquisition in Typical and Neurodiverse Learners*

---

# Executive Summary  
The **Cognitive Trapeze** project investigates how **language and thought co-evolve** within AI-augmented learning environments.  
The project integrates conceptual analysis, cognitive experimentation, multilingual data, and computational modeling to understand how learners navigate between linguistic structures, cognitive representations, and technological mediation.

**Core pedagogical objective:** The project specifically targets **guided reading** in priority education zones (ZEP) in Cameroon. It aims to demonstrate how AI-assisted multimodal tools help learners bridge the gap between **linguistic decoding** and **deep semantic comprehension**.

**Field setting:** 4 pilot schools representing the 4 types of Cameroonian educational institutions (CES, CETIC, General Lycée, Technical Lycée), totaling approximately **160 learners**. All field activities are officially supervised by the **Regional Delegation of MINESEC (Adamaoua)** and its Regional Inspectors (ICR) for IT and Languages.

---

# Project Status (July 2026)

**✅ Host institution and primary supervisor confirmed:** The project is hosted by the **University of Copenhagen (UCPH), Denmark**, under the supervision of **Prof. Daniel Spikol** (Department of Computer Science, DIKU).

**✅ Co‑supervision and secondment confirmed:** Professor **Marcelo Worsley** (Northwestern University, USA) has officially agreed to serve as co‑supervisor for the computational and AI prototyping components, with a **6‑month secondment (M10–M16)** at the *tiilt lab*.

**✅ Field supervision:** The **Regional Delegation of MINESEC (Adamaoua)** and its Regional Inspectors (ICR) will officially supervise all field activities. **Prof. Pius Ondoua** (University of Yaoundé I) acts as academic advisor for the philosophical coherence of the model.

**✅ Technical proof of concept validated:** Over 18 corpus texts have been evaluated (WER) under real classroom conditions. The results validate the choice of **quantized Whisper Large** as the on‑device ASR engine, paired with a lightweight LLM (Qwen 0.5B) for semantic verification. A preprint is being finalized on arXiv (co‑signed with Prof. Worsley).

# PART B1 — EXCELLENCE  

## 1. Project Title  
**Cognitive Trapeze:** Modeling Language–Thought Co‑Development in AI‑Augmented Learning  
*Subtitle: A Computational Approach to Literacy Acquisition in Typical and Neurodiverse Learners*

## 2. Summary  
This project explores how **language and thought co-evolve** within AI-augmented learning environments.  
Building on the *Cognitive Trapeze Model* and the field experience of the **IA4ZEP** programme (supervised by the MINESEC ICRs), it proposes a dynamic computational framework to model how learners move between linguistic structures, cognitive representations, and technological mediation.

## 3. Objectives  

### Scientific objectives  
- Model the dynamic interplay between **language, thought, and technology** via Bayesian networks and multi‑agent simulation.  
- Analyze how AI tools influence conceptual development in reading.  
- Investigate cross‑linguistic and neurodiverse learning patterns (dyslexia, language disorders).  

### Methodological objectives  
- **Field mission 1 (M8–M10):** Baseline assessment (N=160 learners) and preliminary data collection.  
- **Secondment (M10–M16):** MVP APK development at the *tiilt lab* (Northwestern) with Prof. Worsley.  
- **Field mission 2 (M16–M20):** Prototype deployment and pilot test (N=30 learners, 15 dyslexic, 15 typical).  
- **Produce a multilingual dataset** of AI-mediated reading interactions.  

### Career development objectives  
- Strengthen expertise in computational cognitive modeling through the Northwestern secondment.  
- Build a sustainable international research network (Europe–Africa–USA) through collaboration with Prof. Worsley, UCPH, and MINESEC.  
- Publish **4 rank‑A articles** (M8, M16, M22).

# PART B3 — IMPLEMENTATION  

## Work Packages  

### WP1 — Theoretical Foundations (M1–M8)  
Formalization of the Cognitive Trapeze model (Bayesian networks, ABM).  
*Deliverable:* Technical report + **Article 1 (Theoretical)** submitted at M8.  

### WP2 — Empirical Validation (M6–M22)  
- **M8–M10:** Baseline assessment (N=160).  
- **M10–M16:** Researcher is on secondment at Northwestern. No field activities.  
- **M16–M20:** MVP prototype deployment and data collection in the 4 schools. Supervised by MINESEC ICRs.  
- **M20–M22:** Final statistical analysis (Bayesian regression, multilevel models).  
*Deliverables:* **Article 2 (Descriptive)** submitted at M16 + **Article 3 (Confirmatory)** submitted at M22.  

### WP3 — AI Prototyping and Computational Modeling (M10–M22)  
- **M10–M16:** Intensive secondment at Northwestern (*tiilt lab*). Android APK development (quantized Whisper Large + Qwen 0.5B LLM).  
- **M16–M20:** Pilot test of the prototype in the 4 schools (N=30).  
- **M20–M22:** Data analysis and technical paper writing.  
*Deliverables:* Functional alpha APK + **Article 4 (Technical)** submitted at M22 (co‑author Marcelo Worsley).  

### WP4 — Consortium and Sustainability (M15–M24)  
Building a sustainable Africa–Europe partnership (institutional agreements) and ERC Synergy pre‑proposal.  
*Deliverables:* Consortium agreement + ERC pre‑proposal (M23) + dissemination workshop (M24).
